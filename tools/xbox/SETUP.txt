LibrePadPS5 Xbox Wireless manual firmware helper (advanced)

Normal setup uses firmware built into the ELF. Plug in the adapter and pair.
These optional scripts stay in tools/xbox/ in the repository; releases do not
include an Xbox setup ZIP.

To install a firmware override:
1. Load the latest LibrePadPS5 and open http://<PS5_IP>:8096/.
2. Keep setup.cmd and setup.ps1 together, then run setup.cmd.
3. Enter the PS5 IP. The helper downloads Microsoft's driver package, extracts
   the tested image, verifies its SHA-256 and uploads it to the dashboard.
4. Unplug and replug the adapter, then pair your controller.

The override is saved at /data/LibrePadPS5/xbox/firmware.bin and retained across
upgrades. Remove it through FTP and restart the payload to use bundled firmware.
Power-cycle the adapter after changing firmware to clear the resident image.
