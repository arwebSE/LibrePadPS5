LibrePadPS5 Xbox Wireless setup for Windows

1. Load LibrePadPS5 v1.1.0 or newer on the PS5.
2. Confirm http://<PS5_IP>:8096/ opens and reserve your native DualSense profiles.
3. Keep setup.cmd and setup.ps1 together. Double-click setup.cmd.
4. Enter the PS5 IP address. The helper downloads the driver package directly
   from Microsoft, extracts the tested firmware, checks its SHA-256 and installs
   it through the dashboard. No Python, FTP or separate Xbox payload is needed.
5. Unplug and replug the Xbox adapter, then press the adapter and controller
   pairing buttons. Assign the Xbox gamepad to a separate signed-in PS5 profile.

The firmware is saved once and kept across payload upgrades. The receiver may
retain an older firmware image until unplugged; a payload restart alone does
not clear it. The Xbox backend supports one controller per adapter.

If setup fails, read the error shown in the window and check that the dashboard
opens. v1.0.0 and older lack the installation endpoint: load v1.1.0 or newer first.
Alternatively, choose FW_ACC_00U.bin or firmware.bin in Controller > Xbox
Wireless setup > Install firmware. Advanced > Logs & support can download a
diagnostic report to attach to a GitHub issue if you want to share logs.
